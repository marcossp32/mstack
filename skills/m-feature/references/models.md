# Models

| Stage | Model |
|---|---|
| scan A | quickest |
| scan B–D | middle |
| build | most capable |
| prove | most capable |

Quickest, middle and most capable are the harness's own range of models, by its own names. A harness that offers no choice, or a name that does not resolve, runs the agent on the session's model. `/m-research`, `/m-review` and `/m-prove` dispatch their agents on the session's model.
